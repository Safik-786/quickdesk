# New Employee Onboarding

## Before Your First Day

HR will send you a welcome email with:
- Your company email address and temporary password.
- A link to complete your onboarding paperwork in Workday.
- Your manager's contact details.

## Day One Checklist

- [ ] Collect your laptop from IT (or check your desk if remote).
- [ ] Log in and change your temporary password immediately.
- [ ] Set up MFA (Microsoft Authenticator) — instructions in the welcome email.
- [ ] Connect to the company VPN (see VPN Setup article).
- [ ] Complete mandatory compliance training in the Learning Portal (`learn.company.internal`).
- [ ] Book a 1:1 with your manager.

## Accounts and Access

Standard accounts provisioned on day one:
- Company email (Microsoft 365)
- Slack workspace
- Jira / project management tool
- HR system (Workday)

Additional access (code repositories, finance systems, etc.) must be requested via the helpdesk with manager approval.

## IT Equipment

If you did not receive your laptop or any equipment is missing, submit a helpdesk ticket immediately with your employee ID and start date.

## Buddy Program

You will be assigned an onboarding buddy from your team. They are your go-to for informal questions during your first month.

## Questions?

Contact HR at `hr@company.com` for people-related questions, or IT at the helpdesk for technical setup issues.
