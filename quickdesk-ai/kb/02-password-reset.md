# Password Reset Policy

## Self-Service Reset

You can reset your own network/email password at any time via the self-service portal:
**`password.company.internal`**

Requirements for a valid password:
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character
- Cannot reuse your last 10 passwords
- Passwords expire every **90 days** — you will receive email reminders at 14 days and 3 days before expiry.

## Locked Account

After **5 failed login attempts**, your account is locked for 30 minutes automatically. If you need immediate access, contact IT via the helpdesk.

## Forgotten Password (Can't Access Portal)

If you cannot access the self-service portal because you are locked out:
1. Call the IT helpdesk: **ext. 4357** (HELP).
2. IT will verify your identity using your employee ID and manager's name.
3. A temporary password will be issued — you must change it on first login.

## MFA Reset

If you have lost access to your MFA device (phone replacement, etc.), submit a ticket with your employee ID and manager's approval. MFA resets require manager sign-off for security reasons.

## Password Manager

We recommend using **1Password** (company-licensed). Request access through the IT portal.
