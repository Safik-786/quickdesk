# Expense Reimbursement

## What Can Be Reimbursed

- Business travel (flights, hotels, ground transport)
- Client meals and entertainment (with prior manager approval)
- Home office equipment (up to $500/year with manager approval)
- Conference and training fees (pre-approved by manager)
- Software subscriptions required for work (IT approval needed)

## How to Submit an Expense Claim

1. Log in to **Concur** (`concur.company.internal`).
2. Click **Create Expense Report**.
3. Upload receipts for each item (photos are accepted).
4. Select the correct cost centre (ask your manager if unsure).
5. Submit for manager approval.

## Deadlines

- Expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of the purchase date.
- Late submissions require Finance Director approval and may be declined.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are paid in the **next payroll run** (monthly, on the 25th). Submissions approved after the 15th will be paid the following month.

## Limits Without Pre-Approval

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals (per day, travel) | $75 |
| Single item | $250 |
| Hotel (per night) | $200 |

Anything above these limits requires manager pre-approval before purchase.

For questions, contact Finance at `finance@company.com`.
