# Laptop Request and Replacement

## New Laptop Request (New Employees)

New employees receive a laptop as part of onboarding. IT will have it ready on your first day. If you did not receive one, submit a ticket immediately with your start date and manager's name.

## Upgrade or Replacement Request

Laptops are replaced on a **3-year refresh cycle**. To check your eligibility:
1. Go to the IT portal: `it.company.internal/my-devices`.
2. Your device age and refresh eligibility are shown there.

If your laptop is eligible for refresh, submit a ticket with:
- Your current device serial number (found on the sticker on the bottom of the laptop)
- Your preferred OS (Windows or macOS)
- Any specific requirements (e.g., extra RAM for development work)

## Out-of-Cycle Replacement (Hardware Failure)

If your laptop has a hardware fault (broken screen, dead battery, keyboard failure):
1. Submit a helpdesk ticket with a description of the fault.
2. IT will assess within 1 business day.
3. A loan device may be issued while yours is repaired.

## Battery Replacement

Battery replacements are handled by IT. Submit a ticket with your device serial number. Typical turnaround is **2–3 business days**.

## Accessories

Monitors, keyboards, mice, and docking stations can be requested via the IT portal. Standard accessories are approved automatically; premium items require manager approval.
