# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible for remote work. Part-time and contract staff should check their contract terms.

## Hybrid Schedule

The default policy is **3 days in office, 2 days remote** per week. Your manager may adjust this based on team needs. Fully remote arrangements require HR and manager approval.

## Equipment for Remote Work

The company provides:
- Laptop (standard issue)
- VPN access (see VPN Setup article)
- Software licences

Home office equipment (monitor, keyboard, chair) can be expensed up to **$500/year** — see the Expense Reimbursement article.

## Security Requirements

When working remotely you must:
- Always connect via the company VPN for internal resources.
- Use only company-approved devices for work.
- Lock your screen when stepping away.
- Not work from public Wi-Fi without VPN active.
- Report any lost or stolen devices to IT immediately.

## Internet Allowance

A monthly internet allowance of **$30** is available for full-time remote employees. Claim it through Concur under the "Remote Work" category.

## Availability Expectations

Remote employees are expected to be reachable on Slack and email during core hours (**10am–4pm** in your local timezone). Meetings should be scheduled within these hours where possible.

## Questions?

Contact HR at `hr@company.com` for policy questions, or IT for technical setup.
