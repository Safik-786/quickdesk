# Shared Drive and Folder Access

## Requesting Access to a Shared Drive

Access to shared drives (Finance, HR, Legal, etc.) is role-based and requires manager approval.

**To request access:**
1. Submit a helpdesk ticket with:
   - The name of the drive or folder you need access to.
   - The reason you need access.
   - Your manager's name (they will be emailed for approval).
2. Once your manager approves, IT will grant access within **1 business day**.

## Drive Locations

| Drive | Path | Who Has Access |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | `\\fileserver\Finance` | Finance team + approved users |
| HR | `\\fileserver\HR` | HR team only |
| Engineering | `\\fileserver\Engineering` | Engineering team |
| Shared | `\\fileserver\Shared` | All employees |

## Mapping a Network Drive (Windows)

1. Open File Explorer → right-click **This PC** → **Map network drive**.
2. Enter the path (e.g., `\\fileserver\Finance`).
3. Check **Reconnect at sign-in**.
4. Use your company credentials if prompted.

## Google Drive / SharePoint

For cloud storage access, request is made through the IT portal. Access is provisioned within 4 hours during business hours.

## Removing Access

When an employee changes teams or leaves, managers must submit a ticket to revoke access. This is mandatory for compliance.
