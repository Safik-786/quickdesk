# Hardware Repair and Replacement

## Reporting a Hardware Issue

Submit a helpdesk ticket with:
- Device type (laptop, monitor, keyboard, etc.)
- Serial number (on the device label)
- Description of the fault
- How it is affecting your work

## Common Issues and First Steps

**Laptop won't turn on:** Check the charger and try a different power outlet. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a reset.

**Cracked or broken screen:** Do not attempt to use the device — submit a ticket immediately. A loan device will be arranged.

**Keyboard or trackpad not working:** Try restarting first. If the issue persists, an external keyboard/mouse can be issued while the device is repaired.

**Overheating / fan noise:** Ensure vents are not blocked. Run the device on a hard surface. Submit a ticket if it continues.

**Monitor not displaying:** Check cable connections. Try a different cable or port. If the monitor itself is faulty, submit a ticket with the serial number.

## Repair Timeline

| Severity | Response Time |
|---|---|
| Device unusable | Same day loan device + 1–3 days repair |
| Partial fault | 2–5 business days |
| Minor issue | Up to 1 week |

## Out-of-Warranty Devices

Devices over 4 years old may not be repaired — IT will assess whether a replacement is more cost-effective.

## Personal Devices

The company does not repair personally owned devices. If you use a personal device under BYOD policy, contact IT for configuration support only.
