# Leave Application Process

## Types of Leave

| Leave Type | Days Per Year | Notice Required |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Leave | 20 days | 2 weeks |
| Sick Leave | 10 days | Same day (notify manager) |
| Compassionate Leave | 5 days | As soon as possible |
| Unpaid Leave | Discretionary | 4 weeks |

## How to Apply

All leave requests are submitted through **Workday** (`workday.company.internal`):

1. Log in to Workday.
2. Go to **Time Off** → **Request**.
3. Select the leave type and dates.
4. Add a note if needed (required for unpaid leave).
5. Submit — your manager will receive an approval request automatically.

## Approval Timeline

- Annual and unpaid leave: Manager approves within **3 business days**.
- Sick leave: No prior approval needed; notify your manager on the day and log it in Workday within 24 hours.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays are not deducted from your leave balance. The company calendar is published in Workday at the start of each year.

## Carry-Over Policy

Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave can be carried over to the next calendar year. Remaining unused leave is forfeited on December 31st.

For questions about leave balances, contact HR at `hr@company.com`.
