# Printer Setup and Troubleshooting

To connect to an office printer:
1. Open your computer's "Printers & Scanners" settings.
2. Click "Add a printer or scanner".
3. Search for the printer named after your floor (e.g., `Floor-3-Color-Laser`).
4. Click connect. Drivers will install automatically.

If the printer shows "Offline" or fails to print, please try restarting your computer first. If the issue persists, submit an IT ticket with the specific printer name.

To order more toner, please notify the Office Admin team, not IT.
