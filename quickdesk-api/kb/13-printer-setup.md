# Office Printer Setup and Maintenance

## 1. Overview
This guide provides instructions for connecting to networked Multi-Function Printers (MFPs) and utilizing the Secure Print Release system. We use PaperCut for print management and auditing.

## 2. Connecting to an Office Printer
All corporate devices have the universal PaperCut print queue pre-installed. You do not need to install drivers for individual printers.
1. When printing a document, select the printer named **Secure-Print-Global**.
2. Send the print job. It will be held securely in the print server queue for up to 24 hours.
3. Walk up to any MFP in the office.
4. Tap your employee security badge on the RFID reader attached to the printer.
5. Select the document(s) you wish to print from the touchscreen interface and tap **Release**.

## 3. Guest Printing
Guests and contractors without a security badge can use the Web Print feature:
1. Ensure the guest is connected to the **Corp-Guest** Wi-Fi network.
2. Navigate to `https://print.enterprise.com:9192`.
3. Upload the PDF or Office document to the portal.
4. The system will provide a 6-digit release code. Enter this code on the printer's touchscreen.

## 4. Troubleshooting
**Q: I swiped my badge, but the screen says "Unknown User".**
A: Your badge may not be linked to your AD account in the PaperCut database. Submit an IT ticket with your Employee ID and the badge number printed on the back of your ID card.

**Q: The printer is out of toner or paper.**
A: Office Administrators manage paper and toner supplies, not the IT department. Please notify the receptionist or Office Manager on your floor.

**Q: Paper jam.**
A: Follow the animation on the printer's touchscreen to clear the jam. If you cannot clear it or if a part is broken, place an "Out of Order" sign on the machine and submit a ticket to the Facilities team.
