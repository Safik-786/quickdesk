# Shared Drive Access

Access to company shared drives (e.g., Google Drive, SharePoint, internal NAS) is governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

If you receive an "Access Denied" message when trying to open a folder:
1. Verify that you are logged in with your primary company email address.
2. If you still cannot access the folder, submit an IT ticket requesting access.
3. The IT ticket MUST be approved by the "Data Owner" (usually the Department Head of the folder you are trying to access) before IT can grant you permissions.
