# Hardware Procurement: Laptop Request Policy

## 1. Overview
This document specifies the standard procedure for requesting new, replacement, or upgraded laptop hardware for corporate employees. Our hardware lifecycle management policy mandates a hardware refresh every 3 years (36 months) for standard users and every 2 years (24 months) for power users (Engineering/Design).

## 2. Standard Hardware Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Standard Business)**: Lenovo ThinkPad T14 / Dell Latitude 5430 (Intel Core i5, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD). Appropriate for Sales, HR, Finance, and Operations.
- **Tier 2 (Power User)**: Apple MacBook Pro 14" (M3 Pro, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD) or Dell Precision Mobile Workstation. Restricted to Engineering, Product Design, and Data Science.
- **Tier 3 (Executive)**: Lightweight ultrabooks (e.g., Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon or MacBook Air 15").

## 3. Request Procedure
1. Navigate to the **ServiceNow IT Portal**.
2. Select **Service Catalog** > **Hardware** > **Request New Laptop**.
3. Select your assigned Tier based on your role. (Requests for higher tiers require a written business justification and VP approval).
4. Provide the shipping address if you are a remote employee, or select your office location for IT desk pickup.
5. Submit the request. It will automatically route to your cost center manager for financial approval.

## 4. Hardware Replacement (Break/Fix)
If your current laptop is physically damaged, experiencing battery swelling, or suffering critical hardware failures:
- Immediately stop using the device (especially in cases of battery swelling).
- Submit a High Priority IT ticket under **Hardware Repair**.
- A loaner device will be issued within 24 hours while your primary device is evaluated for repair or total replacement.

## 5. Return of Old Hardware
When receiving a replacement laptop, you must return your old device to IT Asset Management within 14 days. Failure to return hardware may result in payroll deductions equivalent to the residual value of the device.

## 6. FAQs
**Q: Can I keep my old laptop if I buy it from the company?**
A: No. Due to data security and compliance regulations, all company hardware must be returned for secure data destruction and e-cycling.
