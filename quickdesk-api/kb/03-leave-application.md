# Employee Leave Application and Time-Off Policy

## 1. Overview
This document outlines the procedure for requesting, approving, and tracking Paid Time Off (PTO), Sick Leave, Bereavement, and unpaid leave using the Workday HRIS portal.

## 2. Accrual and Entitlement
- **PTO**: Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, totaling 18 days annually. Up to 5 days can be rolled over to the next calendar year.
- **Sick Leave**: 10 days of paid sick leave are granted on January 1st of each year. Sick leave does not roll over.
- **Bereavement**: Up to 5 days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.

## 3. Requesting Leave via Workday
1. Log in to **Workday** via the Okta single sign-on dashboard.
2. Navigate to the **Absence** application block.
3. Click **Request Absence**.
4. Select the specific dates on the calendar you wish to take off.
5. In the details panel, select the **Type of Absence** (e.g., PTO, Sick, Jury Duty).
6. Provide an optional comment for your manager.
7. Click **Submit**.

## 4. Approval Workflow
- Requests for 1 to 3 days of PTO require minimum 1 week advance notice and direct manager approval.
- Requests for 4+ consecutive days of PTO require minimum 3 weeks advance notice and both direct manager and department head approval.
- Sick leave can be submitted retroactively but must be logged within 48 hours of returning to work. A doctor's note may be required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## 5. Troubleshooting & HR Support
**Q: My PTO balance looks incorrect.**
A: PTO balances are updated on the 1st of every month. If you recently reached a tenure milestone that increases your accrual rate, it will reflect on the following month's cycle.

**Q: How do I cancel an approved leave request?**
A: In Workday, go to **Absence** > **Correct My Absence**. Select the approved request and click **Cancel**, then submit. This will refund the hours to your balance.

If you experience technical issues with the Workday portal, please submit an IT ticket. For policy questions or balance disputes, contact `hr-benefits@enterprise.com`.
