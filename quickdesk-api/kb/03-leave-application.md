# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Application

All salaried employees receive unlimited PTO. However, PTO requests exceeding 2 consecutive weeks must be approved by your manager and the department VP at least 30 days in advance.

Hourly employees accrue PTO based on hours worked. You can check your accrued PTO balance in the HR portal (Workday).

To request time off:
1. Log in to Workday.
2. Navigate to "Absence".
3. Select the dates and click "Submit for Approval".

If you are sick and cannot work, please notify your manager via Slack and log the hours as "Sick Time" in Workday, not standard PTO.
