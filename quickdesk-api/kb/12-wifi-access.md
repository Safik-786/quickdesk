# Office Wi-Fi Access

The company provides two Wi-Fi networks:
1. **Corp-Secure**: For all company-issued laptops and phones. This network uses certificate-based authentication and should connect automatically. If it does not, contact IT support to have your device certificate renewed.
2. **Corp-Guest**: For personal devices and office visitors. The password changes every Monday and is displayed on the digital screens in the lobby and break rooms.

Please do not connect personal devices to the Corp-Secure network.
