# Remote Work and Telecommuting Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees whose primary work location is designated as "Remote" or who participate in the Hybrid Work program.

## 2. Remote Workspace Requirements
Employees working remotely must maintain a dedicated workspace that meets the following criteria:
- **Internet**: A stable broadband internet connection with a minimum download speed of 50 Mbps and upload speed of 10 Mbps.
- **Privacy**: The workspace must allow for confidential business conversations without being overheard by unauthorized individuals (including family members or roommates).
- **Security**: Corporate devices must be locked when unattended. Personal devices must not be used to access corporate data or networks.

## 3. Remote Work Stipend
Full-time remote employees are eligible for a one-time $500 home office setup stipend, distributed in their first paycheck. This is intended to cover the cost of an ergonomic chair, desk, and external monitor. Additionally, a $50/month internet subsidy can be expensed via Concur.

## 4. Core Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be available via Slack and Zoom during the core business hours of **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM** in their local time zone. Flexibility outside of these core hours must be coordinated with your direct manager.

## 5. Security Protocols
- The use of public Wi-Fi (e.g., coffee shops, airports) requires an active connection to the corporate VPN at all times.
- Printing corporate documents at home or at public print centers is strictly prohibited unless specifically authorized by the Legal department.
- Smart speakers (e.g., Amazon Echo, Google Home) must be muted or removed from the room during confidential meetings.
