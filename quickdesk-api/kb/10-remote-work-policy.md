# Remote Work and WFH Policy

Employees classified as "Remote" or "Hybrid" are eligible to work from home according to their employment contract.

Equipment Stipend:
Remote employees receive a one-time $500 stipend to furnish their home office (desk, chair, lighting). This is submitted via Expensify under the "WFH Stipend" category.
The company will provide all necessary computing hardware (laptop, monitors, docking station) directly.

Security:
When working from home or public spaces, employees must:
- Ensure their screen auto-locks after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Always use the Cisco AnyConnect VPN when accessing internal resources.
- Never connect to unsecured public Wi-Fi networks without the VPN active.
