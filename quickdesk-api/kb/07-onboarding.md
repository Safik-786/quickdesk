# Employee Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

## 1. IT Onboarding Timeline
The IT department requires a minimum of **5 business days** notice prior to a new hire's start date to provision hardware and accounts.

### Day -5: Request Submission
The hiring manager must submit the New Hire Provisioning Form via Workday, specifying the employee role, software requirements, and hardware tier.

### Day -2: Account Creation
IT automatically generates the AD account, email address, and provisions Okta access. Initial credentials are sent to the hiring manager's email.

### Day 1: Orientation
The new hire receives their hardware. They will be prompted to change their temporary password upon first login and enroll their mobile device in MFA.

## 2. Software Provisioning
Standard software (Office 365, Slack, Zoom, Chrome) is pre-installed on all images. Role-specific software (e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud, IDEs) must be specified in the initial provisioning form; otherwise, a separate software request ticket must be submitted post-hire.

## 3. Offboarding Procedure
When an employee departs the organization, the following SLA applies:
- **Voluntary Termination**: Accounts are disabled at 5:00 PM local time on their final day.
- **Involuntary Termination**: Accounts are disabled immediately upon notification from HR.

### Hardware Return
Managers are responsible for ensuring all corporate hardware, security badges, and corporate credit cards are collected prior to the employee's departure.

## 4. Data Retention
The departing employee's email and OneDrive data will be retained for 90 days. A manager can request delegated access to the inbox during this period by submitting a ticket to IT Compliance.
