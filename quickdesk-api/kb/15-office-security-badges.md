# Office Security Badges and Access

All employees must carry their RFID security badge at all times while in the office. 

If you lose your security badge:
1. Report it to IT and Physical Security immediately via an IT ticket or in-person at the front desk.
2. Your lost badge will be deactivated immediately to prevent unauthorized access.
3. A replacement badge will be issued. The first replacement is free; subsequent replacements may incur a $15 replacement fee.

Do not allow guests to "tailgate" behind you through secure doors. All guests must sign in at the front desk and be issued a temporary visitor badge.
