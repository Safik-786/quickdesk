# Office Security Badges and Physical Access

## 1. Overview
Physical security is a critical component of our corporate compliance program. All employees, contractors, and visitors must wear a visible security badge at all times while on company premises. Badges govern access to main entrances, specific floors, server rooms, and secure printing.

## 2. Badge Issuance
- **New Hires**: Badges are issued by the Security/Facilities desk on the first day of employment. A government-issued photo ID is required to claim the badge.
- **Contractors**: Temporary badges are issued upon arrival and must be returned at the end of each workday or contract period.

## 3. Access Levels
Standard employee badges grant access to the main lobby, elevators to designated department floors, and common areas (cafeteria, restrooms) between the hours of 6:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Access to restricted areas (e.g., Server Room, IDF closets, HR Records Room) requires specialized clearance approved by the department head and InfoSec.

## 4. Lost, Stolen, or Forgotten Badges
- **Forgotten Badge**: If you forget your badge at home, visit the Security desk in the main lobby. After verifying your identity, they will issue a temporary 1-day pass. Your permanent badge will be temporarily disabled for the day.
- **Lost/Stolen Badge**: Report a lost or stolen badge **immediately** to the Security Operations Center (SOC) at `security@enterprise.com` or call ext. 9111. The badge will be permanently deactivated. A $25 replacement fee may apply for repeated losses.

## 5. Tailgating Policy
Tailgating or "piggybacking" (allowing someone to follow you through a secure door without swiping their own badge) is strictly prohibited. Every individual must swipe their own badge at every reader. If someone attempts to follow you, politely ask them to swipe their badge.

## 6. Escalation
For issues with badge readers not registering your swipe (blinking red instead of green), submit a ticket to the **Facilities & Physical Security** department. IT does not manage the physical access control systems.
