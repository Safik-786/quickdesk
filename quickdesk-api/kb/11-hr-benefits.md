# HR Benefits Overview and Enrollment

## 1. Enrollment Period
Open enrollment for health, dental, and vision insurance occurs annually during the month of November. Benefit changes take effect on January 1st of the following year. Outside of open enrollment, you may only make changes if you experience a Qualifying Life Event (QLE) such as marriage, birth/adoption of a child, or loss of coverage. You must report a QLE in Workday within 30 days of the event.

## 2. Health Insurance (Medical)
We offer three tiers of medical coverage through BlueCross BlueShield (BCBS):
- **HMO Basic**: Lower premiums, requires primary care physician referrals for specialists.
- **PPO Standard**: Moderate premiums, flexibility to see out-of-network providers.
- **HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan)**: Paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). The company matches up to $1,000 annually into your HSA.

## 3. 401(k) Retirement Plan
Full-time employees are eligible to participate in the Fidelity 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. The company matches 100% of the first 3% of your contributions, and 50% of the next 2%. Contributions can be managed directly via the Fidelity NetBenefits portal.

## 4. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
The EAP provides confidential counseling, legal advice, and financial planning services. Employees and their immediate family members receive up to 6 free counseling sessions per issue, per year. Access the EAP portal at `eap.enterprise.com` or call 1-800-555-0198.

## 5. Contact Information
For specific questions about claims, coverage limits, or provider networks, please contact the BCBS dedicated concierge line. For questions about eligibility or Workday enrollment, email `benefits@enterprise.com`.
