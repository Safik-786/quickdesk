# Email Setup on Mobile Devices

To access company email on your personal or company-issued smartphone, you must use the official Microsoft Outlook app available in the iOS App Store or Google Play Store.

Native mail apps (like Apple Mail) are not supported and will be blocked by the MDM (Mobile Device Management) policy.

1. Download Microsoft Outlook.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. You will be redirected to the company Single Sign-On page.
4. Enter your password and complete the Duo 2FA prompt.

If you lose your phone, please contact IT immediately so we can remotely wipe the company data from the Outlook app.
