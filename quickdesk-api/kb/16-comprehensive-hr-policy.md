# Enterprise Employee Handbook and Global HR Policy

*Adapted from industry-standard open-source employee handbooks (e.g., GitLab, Clef, HackerBay).*

## 1. Introduction and Company Values
Welcome to the team! This handbook is a living document that outlines our shared expectations, global policies, and standard operating procedures.
Our Core Values:
- **Transparency**: Default to public communication internally.
- **Iteration**: Do it in small steps. Ship early, fix often.
- **Inclusion**: Diversity is our strength. We foster an environment where everyone belongs.
- **Results**: We care about what you achieve, not how many hours you sit at your desk.

## 2. Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
### 2.1 Anti-Harassment and Discrimination
We strictly prohibit unlawful discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, color, religion, veteran status, national origin, ancestry, pregnancy status, sex, gender identity or expression, age, marital status, mental or physical disability, or any other characteristics protected by law. 
- **Zero Tolerance**: Substantiated harassment results in immediate termination.
- **Reporting Mechanism**: Report directly to HR, your manager, or via our anonymous whistleblower hotline (`1-800-555-SAFE`). No retaliation will be tolerated against anyone reporting in good faith.

### 2.2 Conflict of Interest & Moonlighting
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with the company's interests. 
- **Moonlighting**: Secondary employment or consulting is permitted only if it does not conflict with the company's business interests, does not use company resources/time, and is pre-approved by the VP of HR.

### 2.3 Social Media Policy
Employees are free to express themselves on social media. However, do not share confidential company information, unreleased product details, or represent your personal opinions as the official stance of the company.

## 3. Employment Classifications
- **Regular Full-Time**: Scheduled to work 40 hours per week. Eligible for all global benefits.
- **Regular Part-Time**: Scheduled to work between 20 and 39 hours per week. Eligible for prorated benefits.
- **Temporary / Contractor**: Engaged for a specific project or time period. Not eligible for standard corporate benefits unless required by local law.

## 4. Working Hours and Location
### 4.1 Flexible Working Hours
We operate asynchronously. While departments may establish "core hours" for sync meetings (e.g., 10 AM to 2 PM PST), employees are generally trusted to manage their own schedules provided they meet their goals and do not block their teammates.
### 4.2 Remote-First Policy
Employees may work from anywhere with a reliable internet connection. If relocating internationally for more than 30 days, you must notify HR Operations to ensure payroll and tax compliance.

## 5. Compensation and Payroll
- **Pay Periods**: Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and the final business day of the month.
- **Overtime**: Non-exempt employees must obtain manager approval prior to working overtime. Overtime is paid at 1.5x the standard hourly rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek.
- **Expense Reimbursement**: Business expenses must be submitted via Concur within 30 days of the receipt date.

## 6. Time Off and Leaves of Absence
### 6.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
We operate on a "Flexible PTO" (unlimited) model for exempt employees. 
- **Guideline**: We encourage all employees to take a minimum of 25 days off per year to recharge.
- **Approval**: Requests under 3 days require notifying your manager. Requests of 4+ days require minimum 2 weeks' notice and manager approval to ensure coverage.
### 6.2 Sick Leave
If you are sick, do not work. Sick leave is fully paid. If an illness extends beyond 5 consecutive days, HR may request a doctor's note to transition to Short-Term Disability (STD).
### 6.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 8 weeks. This applies to birth, adoption, and foster placements.
### 6.4 Compassionate / Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 10 days of paid leave following the death of an immediate family member, or 3 days for an extended family member.
### 6.5 Sabbatical
After 5 years of continuous service, employees are eligible for a 6-week paid sabbatical to pursue personal projects, travel, or rest. 

## 7. Performance Management
- **Continuous Feedback**: Managers are expected to hold weekly 1:1s.
- **Bi-Annual Reviews**: Formal 360-degree reviews occur in Q1 and Q3. 
- **Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)**: If an employee consistently falls below expectations, they will be placed on a 30, 60, or 90-day PIP with clear, measurable goals. Failure to pass the PIP will result in termination.

## 8. Disciplinary Policy
Infractions of policy may result in progressive discipline:
1. Documented Verbal Warning.
2. Written Warning.
3. Final Written Warning / Suspension.
4. Termination of Employment.
*Note: Gross misconduct (e.g., theft, violence, severe harassment) warrants immediate termination, bypassing progressive discipline.*

## 9. Separation of Employment (Notice Period)
### 9.1 Voluntary Resignation
If you choose to leave the company, we ask for standard notice to ensure a smooth handover:
- **Individual Contributors**: 2 weeks (14 days) notice.
- **Managers / Directors**: 4 weeks (28 days) notice.
- **VPs and C-Level**: 8 weeks (56 days) notice.
Unused Flexible PTO is not paid out upon resignation unless strictly required by local state/country law.

### 9.2 Involuntary Termination
In the event of termination without cause (e.g., layoffs, role elimination), employees will be provided with a severance package based on tenure (minimum 4 weeks base pay) and transition assistance.

## 10. IT, Security, and Asset Management
### 10.1 Acceptable Use
Corporate laptops and networks are for business purposes. Modest personal use is permitted provided it does not violate security policies or consume excessive bandwidth.
### 10.2 Security Badges
Employees must wear security badges visibly while in any corporate office. Lost badges must be reported to `security@enterprise.com` immediately.
### 10.3 Equipment Return
Upon separation of employment, all corporate assets (laptops, monitors, phones, badges) must be returned to IT within 7 business days via the provided prepaid shipping box.
