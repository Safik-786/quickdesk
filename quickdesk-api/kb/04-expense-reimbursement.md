# Corporate Expense and Travel Reimbursement

## 1. Overview
This policy details the guidelines and procedures for submitting corporate expenses incurred during business travel, client entertainment, or procurement of approved office supplies. All reimbursements are processed through the Concur Expense platform.

## 2. Eligible Expenses
- **Travel**: Economy class airfare, standard hotel accommodations (up to $250/night), and ground transportation (rideshares, taxis, standard rental cars).
- **Meals**: Per diem allowance of $75/day for domestic travel and $100/day for international travel. Itemized receipts are required for all meals.
- **Client Entertainment**: Must include the names of all attendees, company affiliation, and the specific business purpose discussed.
- **Home Office**: Up to $50/month internet stipend for fully remote employees.

## 3. Non-Eligible Expenses
- First-class or business-class airfare (unless approved by VP-level or above for international flights > 8 hours).
- Alcohol (unless part of approved client entertainment).
- Personal grooming, parking tickets, or traffic violations.

## 4. Submission Process (Concur)
1. Log in to **SAP Concur** via the Okta dashboard or the Concur mobile app.
2. Click **Create New Report** and enter the Report Name (e.g., "Q3 Client Summit - NYC") and Business Purpose.
3. Click **Add Expense** to manually enter details or use the **Available Receipts** gallery to attach photos of your receipts.
4. For expenses over $25.00, a clear, itemized receipt is strictly mandatory.
5. Once all expenses are added, click **Submit Report**.

## 5. Reimbursement Timeline
- Reports submitted by Friday 5:00 PM EST will be reviewed by Finance the following week.
- Approved expenses will be disbursed via direct deposit to your primary payroll account within 7-10 business days of final approval.

## 6. Audits and Escalation
Finance conducts random audits on 10% of all expense reports. Discrepancies may result in delayed payment or disciplinary action. For questions regarding a rejected expense report, please consult the comments left by the auditor in Concur or email `ap-expenses@enterprise.com`.
