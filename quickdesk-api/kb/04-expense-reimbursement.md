# Expense Reimbursement Process

Business-related expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction via Expensify.

Required documentation:
- An itemized receipt must be attached for any expense over $25.
- Credit card statements are not accepted in place of an itemized receipt.
- Meals with clients must include the names of all attendees and the business purpose of the meal in the expense description.

Reimbursements are processed on the 15th and 30th of each month and will be paid out via direct deposit alongside standard payroll. If your expense report is rejected by Finance, you will receive a notification in Expensify detailing what needs to be corrected.
