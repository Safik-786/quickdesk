# Hardware Repair and Break/Fix

If your company-issued hardware (laptop, phone, monitor) physically breaks or malfunctions:

1. Submit a Hardware Repair ticket immediately. Do not attempt to repair the device yourself.
2. If working in the office, bring the device to the IT Helpdesk on the 2nd floor.
3. If working remotely, IT will send you a pre-paid shipping box to mail the device to our repair center.

A temporary "loaner" laptop will be issued to you while your primary device is being repaired. If the device was accidentally damaged (e.g., liquid spill, dropped), it will be replaced but may require a department chargeback depending on the frequency of incidents.
