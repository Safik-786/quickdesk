# Hardware Repair and Break/Fix Policy

## 1. Overview
The IT Support team is responsible for maintaining the operational status of all corporate-issued hardware, including laptops, monitors, docking stations, and peripherals. 

## 2. Triage and Initial Troubleshooting
Before submitting a repair request for an unresponsive device, please perform a hard reset:
- **Windows (Lenovo/Dell)**: Hold the power button for 30 seconds. Wait 5 seconds, then press the power button once to turn it on.
- **macOS**: Hold the Touch ID/power button for 10 seconds. Release, then press again to turn on.

## 3. Submitting a Hardware Ticket
If the issue persists, submit a ticket via the IT Helpdesk:
1. Select the **Hardware Issue** category.
2. Include the device's Asset Tag (found on the barcode sticker on the bottom).
3. Describe the issue in detail (e.g., "Screen flickers when adjusting the hinge," "Fan is extremely loud," "Trackpad not registering clicks").
4. If there is visible physical damage, attach a clear photo.

## 4. SLA and Loaner Devices
- **Critical Failure (Unable to work)**: If a device is completely dead, IT will issue a loaner device within 4 business hours (for office employees) or ship a replacement overnight (for remote employees).
- **Non-Critical Failure (e.g., broken USB port)**: IT will schedule a repair window. Repairs may take 3-5 business days.

## 5. Warranty and Accidental Damage
Most corporate devices are covered by a 3-year OEM warranty with Accidental Damage Protection. However, gross negligence (e.g., leaving a laptop on the roof of a car) may result in departmental chargebacks for the replacement cost. Liquid spills should be reported immediately, and the device should be powered off instantly to prevent short-circuiting.
